Pricing Transparency

How Your Campaign Investment Is Determined

The final investment for every campaign is calculated based on a set of specific factors that are unique to each project. Understanding these factors helps authors plan their budget before submitting an enquiry.

01

Campaign Scope and Services Required

The number of services, channels, and marketing activities included in the campaign directly affects the investment. A single-channel promotion costs less than a coordinated multi-platform campaign.

02

Campaign Duration and Timeline

Longer campaigns that require sustained execution, ongoing optimization, and extended monitoring require a proportionally higher investment than short, concentrated promotional bursts.

03

Advertising Budget Requirements

Paid advertising campaigns (Amazon Ads, Meta, TikTok) require an advertising budget that is separate from the agency's management fee. Both are clearly outlined in the campaign proposal.

04

Genre, Market and Audience Complexity

The competitiveness of the book's genre, the size of the target audience, and the platform landscape all influence the strategy required. And therefore the investment needed to execute it effectively.

05

Author's Existing Marketing Foundation

Authors with established platforms, existing audiences, and optimized Amazon listings require less foundational setup. Which can reduce the total campaign investment compared to authors starting from zero.

06

Campaign Objectives and Performance Targets

A campaign focused on awareness requires a different scope than one targeting chart rankings or a specific sales number. The objective defines the strategy, and the strategy defines the investment.

Important to Understand

What Your Marketing Investment Covers

Book marketing investment includes two distinct cost types. Both are clearly separated in every proposal.

Agency Fee

The fee paid to Albert Nathan Agency for strategy development, campaign management, execution, optimization, reporting, and direct communication throughout the project.

Advertising Spend

The budget paid directly to advertising platforms (Amazon, Meta, TikTok) for the actual ad delivery. This is separate from and in addition to the agency fee and is always clearly defined upfront.

Transparent Proposal

Every proposal separates these costs clearly so authors know exactly what they are paying for. Agency work, advertising investment, and any third-party tools required for the campaign.

Payment Terms

Payment terms are confirmed in the proposal. Campaigns typically require a deposit before work begins, with the remainder invoiced at agreed milestones or on completion.

Ongoing vs. One-Time

Some campaigns are one-time projects with a fixed total investment. Ongoing management campaigns (monthly advertising management, social media, etc.) are billed on a recurring basis as agreed.

No Commitment Without a Proposal

Submitting an enquiry carries no financial obligation. A proposal is prepared and presented for your review before any agreement is entered into or any investment is requested.

Pricing Questions

Frequently Asked Pricing Questions

What is the minimum investment for a book marketing campaign?
The minimum campaign investment at Albert Nathan Agency is $500. This covers focused, single-channel promotional campaigns with a specific, defined objective. For broader multi-channel campaigns, the recommended minimum is $2,500+. The exact figure for your project is confirmed in your custom proposal.
Do the investment levels include advertising spend?
The investment levels shown are the agency's management and strategy fees. If your campaign includes paid advertising (Amazon Ads, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok), the advertising budget is separate. Paid directly to the platform and clearly outlined in your proposal alongside the agency fee. Both costs are always transparently presented before any commitment.
Can I choose specific services rather than a full campaign?
Yes. Individual services such as Amazon listing optimization, influencer outreach, a book review campaign, or a social media strategy can be commissioned independently. The investment for individual services is agreed based on the scope and workload of that specific service. When you enquire, tell us exactly what you need and a tailored proposal is prepared.
Is a deposit required before the campaign begins?
Yes. All campaigns require a deposit before work begins. The deposit amount and remaining payment schedule are clearly outlined in your campaign proposal. The payment terms are agreed upon and confirmed in writing before any campaign activity starts.
Do you offer payment plans?
Payment structure is discussed as part of the proposal process. For longer campaigns, milestone-based payments may be arranged. This is confirmed in writing before any work begins. Contact us to discuss what works for your situation.
How do I know which investment level is right for my book?
Submit your enquiry and Albert Nathan will personally review your book, current marketing position, and objectives. Then recommend the most appropriate campaign scope for your budget and goals. You are not obligated to accept any recommendation. The process is designed to help you make an informed decision before committing to anything.
What happens if my project does not fit the standard levels?
Custom campaigns are available for projects that do not fit the standard investment levels. Whether that is a smaller, very focused need below $500, or a large-scale publisher campaign above $20,000. Every project is assessed individually. Contact us and describe your project. A tailored response will follow.
Is there any obligation when I submit an enquiry?
No. Submitting an enquiry is completely free and carries no financial obligation. Albert Nathan reviews your project, prepares a tailored proposal, and presents it for your consideration. You are free to review it, ask questions, and decide whether to proceed on your own timeline, without any pressure.

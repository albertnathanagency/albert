The Beginning

Albert Nathan did not set out to become a book marketing strategist. He started where most people in publishing start. With a genuine, almost unreasonable love of books and the belief that the right story, in the right hands, has the power to change how someone sees the world.

What he discovered early in his career was a persistent, frustrating gap: authors with genuinely exceptional books who had no idea how to make those books visible. Great writing was being published into silence. Debut authors were spending years crafting something meaningful and then watching it disappear into a market of millions with no marketing strategy behind it.

The Work

Over ten years of working across book promotion, digital advertising, Amazon marketing, author branding, reader outreach, and campaign strategy, Albert developed a methodology that treats every book as its own marketing challenge. Not a template to be filled in.

He has worked with debut authors finding their first readers, with self-published authors building serious commercial traction, with traditionally published authors who needed marketing support their publishers could not fully provide, and with publishers who needed a specialist to execute campaigns their internal teams were not equipped to deliver.

Every campaign has taught him something. Every book has required a different approach. The consistent thread across all of it has been the same: strategy first, execution second, measurement throughout.

Why He Started the Agency

Albert Nathan Agency was built on a simple observation. Most book marketing services were either too generic, too expensive for independent authors, or disconnected from the reality of how modern readers discover books.

He wanted to build something different. A solo agency where authors work directly with the person running their campaign. No handoffs, no account managers, no diluted communication. Just experienced, strategic, personally executed book marketing.

What Drives Him

The moment that drives Albert Nathan is not a campaign metric. It is the moment an author tells him their book found the audience it was always meant for. That a reader reached out to say the book changed something for them. That a debut novel that might have gone unnoticed was discovered by someone who needed exactly that story.

That is why this agency exists. And that is why every campaign it runs is treated as if the book is the only book that matters.

Marketing Philosophy

What Albert Nathan Believes About Book Marketing

These are not taglines. They are the principles that shape every campaign, every strategy, and every decision made inside Albert Nathan Agency.

Strategy Before Tactics

Running ads without strategy is expensive noise. Posting on social media without a plan is activity without purpose. Every campaign must begin with a clear understanding of the book, the audience, and the objective. Then the tactics follow.

Readers Are the Market

Marketing is not about impressing the industry. It is about reaching the specific humans who will love this book. Everything, the messaging, the targeting, the channels, is built around where those readers actually are and how they actually discover books.

Data Should Drive Decisions

Gut feeling has its place at the beginning of a campaign. After that, data takes over. What is working gets more investment. What is not gets cut or changed. Marketing that is not measured is not marketing. It is spending.

Books Need Sustained Visibility

A single launch event is not a marketing strategy. The books that build lasting commercial performance are the ones with consistent, sustained visibility across multiple channels over time. Not one spike followed by silence.

Transparency Is Non-Negotiable

Authors deserve to know exactly what is being done with their investment, what it is producing, and what the honest assessment of their campaign is. No inflated promises. No vague reporting. Straight answers at every stage.

Every Book Is Unique

No two books have the same audience, the same competitive landscape, or the same marketing opportunity. Applying the same template to every campaign is the fastest way to produce average results. Custom strategy is the only approach that makes sense.

The Approach

How Albert Nathan Builds a Campaign

The same disciplined process applied to every book, regardless of genre, budget, or publishing path.

01

Understand the Book

Before any strategy is written, the book is studied. The genre, the audience, the competitive titles, the positioning, the unique selling points. All of it informs the campaign direction.

02

Audit What Exists

A review of the author's current marketing presence, platform, Amazon listing, social presence, email list, and any previous promotional activity. What is working. What is missing. What is hurting.

03

Define the Objective

What is this campaign for? Sales? Visibility? Reviews? Audience growth? Rankings? A clear, honest objective shapes every decision that follows.

04

Build the Strategy

A tailored marketing plan is developed, channels, messaging, timeline, budget allocation, and execution sequence. All specific to this book and this objective.

05

Execute with Precision

The campaign is launched and managed personally by Albert Nathan. Every platform, every ad set, every outreach message. Executed with attention and intent.

06

Measure, Report and Optimize

Performance is monitored throughout. Reporting is transparent. Campaigns are adjusted based on what the data shows, not assumptions.

Step-by-Step

The Complete Process

From the moment you reach out to the launch of your campaign, you will know exactly where you are in the process.

01
Stage One

You Enquire

You submit your book details and marketing objectives through the enquiry form on our website, WhatsApp, or directly by email. No pressure. No long questionnaire. Just the essential information so we understand your book and your goals.

Timeline: Immediate
What we need: Book title, genre, publishing date, marketing goals, budget
Next: We respond within 24 to 48 hours
02
Stage Two

We Review Your Book

Albert Nathan personally reviews your book, your current marketing presence (if any), and the competitive landscape in your genre. We assess your positioning, audience potential, and what marketing opportunities exist.

Timeline: 2 to 3 business days
What we analyze: Book quality, genre competitiveness, pricing, audience fit
Next: You hear from us with initial impressions
03
Stage Three

We Build Your Strategy

Based on the review, we develop a tailored marketing strategy specific to your book and goals. Which channels will work best? What audience should we target? How long should the campaign run? What will success look like?

Timeline: 3 to 5 business days
What you will have: A clear roadmap designed specifically for your book
Next: We prepare your proposal
04
Stage Four

You Receive Your Proposal

We send a detailed proposal that includes the strategy, recommended services, campaign timeline, deliverables, and the investment required. No surprises. Everything is clear and justified.

Timeline: Delivered within 1 week of strategy completion
What is included: Strategy, timeline, services, investment, what to expect
Next: You review and ask questions
05
Stage Five

We Discuss and Refine

You have questions. We have answers. You might want to adjust the scope. We will explore it. If the proposal does not feel right, we will discuss alternatives. No pressure to move forward. Only clarity.

Timeline: As long as you need
What happens: Honest conversation about what is best for your book
Next: You decide to move forward (or not)
06
Stage Six

Agreement and Payment

You approve the proposal. We send a signed agreement. You pay the deposit (or full investment for shorter campaigns). Everything is documented and clear.

Timeline: Within 24 hours of approval
What happens: Formal agreement signed, payment received
Next: Your campaign begins
07
Stage Seven

Campaign Execution Begins

Albert Nathan personally manages every element of your campaign. Platform optimization, ad creation, audience targeting, influencer outreach, social media. Every part of the strategy is brought to life.

Timeline: Begins immediately upon payment
What happens: Full execution across all agreed channels
Next: Regular monitoring and communication
08
Stage Eight

Monitoring and Optimization

Throughout the campaign, we monitor performance, track results, and optimize as needed. If something is not working, we adjust. If something is working, we amplify it.

Timeline: Ongoing throughout campaign
What happens: Regular check-ins, adjustments, optimization
Next: Regular reporting and updates
09
Stage Nine

Campaign Reporting

You receive clear, honest reporting on what the campaign achieved. What worked. What did not. What the numbers mean. No fluff, no jargon. Just transparency.

Timeline: Regular intervals (weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly)
What you get: Clear metrics, performance breakdown, insights
Next: Campaign conclusion or extension
Key Expectations

What You Should Expect Working With Us

Direct Access

You work directly with Albert Nathan. Not an account manager. Not a junior strategist. The person running your campaign is the person you speak with.

Custom Strategy

No templates. No standard packages. Your campaign is built specifically for your book, your audience, your goals, and your timeline.

Measurable Results

We track everything. You will know what the campaign achieved, what the spend produced, and where your visibility improved.

Open Communication

No surprise invoices. No hidden processes. You know exactly what is being done, when, why, and what it costs.

Timeline Honesty

We do not promise overnight results. We explain what is realistic, how long it typically takes, and what you can expect when.

No Pressure

If our approach does not feel right for you, we say so. We work with authors we believe we can genuinely help, not every author.

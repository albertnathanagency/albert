Everything You Need to Know

If you have a question about how Albert Nathan Agency works, what we offer, how much it costs, or what to expect, you will find the answer here.

General Questions
What is Albert Nathan Agency?
Albert Nathan Agency is a book marketing agency founded by Albert Nathan. We help authors, publishers, and publishing professionals build visibility, reach more readers, and create strategic marketing campaigns around their books. Every campaign is personally executed by Albert Nathan. No delegation, no outsourcing.
What makes Albert Nathan Agency different from other book marketing agencies?
There are three key differences. First, you work directly with Albert Nathan. Not a team of junior account managers. Second, every campaign is custom-built for your specific book and goals. No templates. Third, we are transparent about what we do, what it costs, and what results are realistic. No inflated promises. No hidden processes.
Do you work with authors outside the USA?
Yes. Albert Nathan Agency works with authors and publishers worldwide. Campaigns are delivered internationally, and we target the markets that are most relevant to your book and genre. Location is never a barrier.
What genres do you work with?
All genres. Fiction, nonfiction, children's, thriller, romance, literary fiction, fantasy, science fiction, self-help, business, memoir, biography, and specialist titles. The marketing strategy is adapted to the specific genre, audience, and publishing platform of your book.
Do you work with debut authors?
Yes. Debut authors are welcome. Building a strong marketing foundation from the very first book is one of the most valuable investments an author can make. We work with authors at every stage, from first-time publishing to established multi-title authors.
Pricing and Investment
How much does a book marketing campaign cost?
Campaign investment starts at $500 and scales based on scope, channels, objectives, and timeline. Every campaign receives a custom proposal with a clear breakdown of investment and deliverables. There are no hidden fees. The exact cost for your project is confirmed after reviewing your book and goals.
Do I need to have a set budget before contacting you?
Not necessarily. If you have a budget range in mind, that helps us scope the campaign appropriately. But if you are not sure what is realistic, we can discuss that during the enquiry process and recommend a campaign scope that fits your objectives and what you are comfortable investing.
Is there a minimum investment?
The minimum campaign investment is $500. This covers focused, single-channel promotional campaigns with a specific, defined objective. For broader multi-channel campaigns, the recommended minimum is $2,500+. The exact figure is confirmed in your custom proposal.
Do the investment levels include advertising spend?
The investment levels shown are the agency's management and strategy fees. If your campaign includes paid advertising (Amazon Ads, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok), the advertising budget is separate and paid directly to the platform. Both costs are clearly outlined in your proposal before any commitment.
Do you offer payment plans?
Payment structure is discussed as part of the proposal process. For longer campaigns, milestone-based payments may be arranged. This is confirmed in writing before any work begins. Contact us to discuss what works for your situation.
Is there any obligation when I submit an enquiry?
No. Submitting an enquiry is completely free and carries no financial obligation. Albert Nathan reviews your project, prepares a tailored proposal, and presents it for your consideration. You are free to review it, ask questions, and decide whether to proceed on your own timeline without any pressure.
Services and Process
What services does Albert Nathan Agency offer?
The full range of book marketing services: book launch campaigns, Amazon marketing, author branding, paid advertising (Amazon, Meta, TikTok), reader outreach, influencer and blogger outreach, social media marketing, SEO, email marketing, Goodreads promotion, consulting, and custom campaigns. See the Services page for full details.
Can I choose specific services rather than a full campaign?
Yes. Individual services such as Amazon listing optimization, influencer outreach, a book review campaign, or a social media strategy can be commissioned independently. The investment for individual services is agreed based on the scope and workload of that specific service.
What happens after I submit an enquiry?
Your enquiry is personally reviewed by Albert Nathan. You receive a response within 24 to 48 hours. If your project is a good fit, we discuss your book, marketing objectives, and campaign options. A tailored proposal is then prepared for your review.
How long does a book marketing campaign take?
It depends on the campaign scope. A book launch campaign typically runs for 4 to 12 weeks with the core intensity around the release window. Sustained marketing over 6 to 12 months consistently outperforms one-time campaigns. The most successful authors treat marketing as an ongoing activity, not a single event.
Do you work with self-published authors?
Yes. Self-published authors are a significant part of who we work with. We understand the specific challenges independent authors face and build campaigns designed to help them compete effectively in the commercial marketplace.
Do you work with traditionally published authors?
Yes. We work with authors who have traditional publishing deals and need marketing support beyond what their publisher's in-house team is able to provide. We also work directly with publishers.
Do you work with publishers?
Yes. Independent and mid-size publishers who need specialist book marketing support for individual titles or across their publishing catalogue are welcome. We can provide title-specific campaigns or multi-title marketing programs.
Results and Expectations
Do you guarantee book sales?
No. Book sales are influenced by many factors including book quality, cover design, pricing, reader demand, competition, timing, and market conditions. Many of these fall outside the scope of marketing. What we guarantee is strategic, professional execution of the agreed campaign. Marketing results vary and no specific outcome can be promised.
How quickly can I see marketing results?
Some marketing channels produce early results within days. Paid advertising, for example, can drive traffic quickly. Other channels such as SEO, organic audience building, and outreach campaigns take longer to develop. A realistic timeline for sustained marketing results is typically 4 to 12 weeks depending on the campaign scope and starting position.
Do you provide campaign reports?
Yes. Every campaign includes clear, honest reporting on what was executed and what the campaign produced. Reports are provided at regular intervals (weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly depending on the campaign scope). You always know where your investment is going and what it is producing.
Will I work directly with Albert Nathan?
Yes. Albert Nathan Agency is a solo agency. Every author works directly with Albert Nathan throughout the entire process. From enquiry to proposal to campaign execution and reporting. No account managers. No junior staff. Direct accountability throughout.
What happens if I am not satisfied with the campaign?
We have honest conversations throughout the campaign. If something is not working, we adjust. If the campaign is not meeting expectations, we discuss why and explore alternatives. Our goal is always to deliver the best possible outcome for your book, and we remain accountable to that throughout the project.
Do you have case studies or testimonials I can see?
Yes. You can view real case studies and author testimonials on this website. All case studies and testimonials are from real authors and campaigns, published with permission.
Technical and Requirements
Do I need to have an author website?
Having a professional author website is strongly recommended. It is the central hub for your author brand and online presence. If you do not have one, we can help you build it as part of your campaign. A website is not an absolute requirement, but it significantly improves your marketing results.
Do I need an existing audience?
No. Many authors start with no existing audience. Part of what we do is help you build an audience and reader community from the ground up. That said, having an existing audience does give you a head start, and we can help you leverage it more effectively.
Do I need to be on Amazon to work with you?
Most of our campaigns include Amazon marketing because it is the world's largest book marketplace. However, we work with authors selling through other platforms as well. If you are not on Amazon, we can still build a campaign around the channels that are relevant to your book.

The Challenge Every Author Faces

Great Books Don't Always Find Their Readers

You have invested everything into writing your book. The words are there. The story is real. But without the right marketing infrastructure behind it, even the most powerful books remain invisible in an overcrowded market.

Poor Visibility Your book exists but readers cannot find it among millions of titles
Weak Discoverability No Amazon presence, no search ranking, no pathway to organic discovery
Limited Audience Reach Marketing that does not reach the specific readers who would love your book
Unfocused Campaigns Random promotional activity with no strategy and no measurable results
No Author Brand Publishing without a professional brand that builds trust with readers

The market has never been more competitive.

Over four million books are published every year. Readers are overwhelmed with choice. The authors who succeed are not always the best writers. They are the most strategically marketed ones.

Visibility is not accidental. Discovery is engineered. A successful book marketing campaign requires deep knowledge of publishing platforms, reader psychology, advertising systems, author positioning, and multi-channel execution.

Frequently Asked Questions

Ask a Question
How much does book marketing cost?
Book marketing investment with Albert Nathan Agency starts at $500 and scales based on campaign scope, the channels used, your marketing objectives, and your timeline. Every campaign receives a custom proposal with a clear breakdown of investment and deliverables. There are no hidden fees.
Do you guarantee book sales?
No. Book sales are influenced by many factors including book quality, cover design, pricing, reader demand, competition, timing, and market conditions. Many of these fall outside the scope of marketing. What we guarantee is strategic, professional execution of the agreed campaign. Marketing results vary and no specific outcome can be promised.
How quickly can I see marketing results?
Some marketing channels produce early results within days. Paid advertising, for example, can drive traffic quickly. Other channels such as SEO, organic audience building, and outreach campaigns take longer to develop. A realistic timeline for sustained marketing results is typically 4 to 12 weeks depending on the campaign scope and starting position.
Do you work with debut authors?
Yes. Debut authors are welcome. In fact, building a strong marketing foundation from the very first book is one of the most valuable investments an author can make in their writing career. We work with authors at every stage, from first-time publishing to established multi-title authors.
Do you work with self-published and traditionally published authors?
Yes to both. Whether your book is independently published, traditionally published, or published through a hybrid model, Albert Nathan Agency can build and execute a marketing campaign around it. We also work with publishers directly.
Do you market books internationally?
Yes. Albert Nathan Agency works with authors and publishers worldwide. Campaigns are delivered internationally with audience targeting focused on the markets that are most relevant to your book and genre.
What genres do you work with?
All genres. Fiction, nonfiction, children's, thriller, romance, literary fiction, fantasy, science fiction, self-help, business, memoir, biography, and specialist titles. Marketing strategy is adapted to the specific genre, audience, and publishing platform.
What is the minimum marketing budget?
The minimum campaign investment with Albert Nathan Agency is $500. This covers targeted, focused campaigns. For broader multi-channel campaigns, the recommended minimum is $2,500+. Budget requirements are discussed during the initial enquiry process.
How long should I market my book?
There is no single answer. A book launch campaign typically runs for 4 to 12 weeks with the core intensity around the release window. However, sustained marketing over 6 to 12 months consistently outperforms one-time campaigns. The most successful authors treat marketing as an ongoing activity, not a single event.
What happens after I submit an enquiry?
Your enquiry is personally reviewed by Albert Nathan. You will receive a response within 24 to 48 hours. If your project is a good fit, we will discuss your book, marketing objectives, and campaign options. A tailored proposal is then prepared for your review.
Can I combine multiple services?
Yes, and we recommend it. The most effective book marketing campaigns combine multiple coordinated channels. Advertising, outreach, social media, Amazon optimization, and author branding working together. Multi-channel campaigns consistently outperform single-service promotion.
